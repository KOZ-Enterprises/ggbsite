Hi, I'm Gary — an engineer, builder, and eternal student of the universe. With a background in mechanical and aerospace engineering and a deepening focus on computer science, I explore the intersections of hardware, software, and science.

This site is a home for my hobby and academic projects — from autonomous Arduino rovers and Raspberry Pi clusters to quantum computing simulations and full-stack web apps. Whether I’m wiring up sensors, deploying services in Docker, or modeling complex systems, I approach every project with curiosity, creativity, and a desire to make things that matter.

I believe in learning by building — and breaking — and rebuilding better.
