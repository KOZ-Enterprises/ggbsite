About Me

Hi, I'm Gary — an engineer, builder, and lifelong learner with a passion for the future of autonomous systems. With a background in mechanical and aerospace engineering and a growing expertise in computer science, I've found my calling at the intersection of hardware, software, and real-world problem-solving: autonomous drone swarms and distributed robotics.

My Journey

I hold a Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering and am currently pursuing a Computer Science degree at California State University, Monterey Bay (CSUMB). This unique combination gives me a holistic perspective on how mechanical systems and intelligent software come together to create the next generation of robotics.

What Drives Me

I'm fascinated by the potential of fleets of drones working together — from creating mesmerizing light shows to coordinating disaster response and search-and-rescue operations. Drone swarms are where mechanical design, advanced algorithms, and AI-driven collaboration meet. This field lets me combine hands-on engineering with the creativity of software and the challenge of large-scale, real-world impact.

How I Work

This site documents my journey: from building Arduino rovers and Raspberry Pi clusters to experimenting with machine learning, quantum computing simulations, and distributed autonomous systems. Whether I'm wiring up sensors, developing swarm algorithms, deploying in Docker, or modeling complex behaviors, I approach each project with the mindset: learn by building, breaking, and rebuilding better.

Beyond the Code

When I'm not coding or tinkering with hardware, you’ll find me diving into new developments in AI, contributing to open-source projects, or sketching out the next experiment in robotics. I believe technology should solve real problems and make a positive impact on the world.

Let's Connect

I’m always excited to meet fellow engineers, developers, and curious minds. If you’d like to collaborate, discuss autonomous systems, or just geek out about robotics and technology, feel free to reach out via LinkedIn or check out my latest work here.
