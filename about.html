About Me

Hi, I’m Gary—an engineer, builder, and lifelong tinkerer obsessed with complex systems. I started in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, spending over a decade in the industry, currently working at Boeing on real-world aircraft programs.

But I realized my favorite problems were always the algorithmic ones: simulations, automation, and control logic. That’s why I went back for a second degree in Computer Science at CSUMB. I’m not leaving engineering behind; I’m combining it with CS to live at the intersection of hardware, software, and real-world impact—building tangible products, autonomous systems, and practical AI applications.

What Drives Me

I’m fascinated by systems of agents—fleets of drones coordinating search-and-rescue, swarms forming shapes on the fly, or decentralized robots adapting without a central controller. For me, this is the sweet spot: hard engineering + advanced algorithms.

My focus is on decentralized control, computer vision, and learning-based coordination to make machines smarter, more collaborative, and more capable in the physical world.

About This Site

This site is my public lab notebook. It's where I document the tangible products I'm building, from Arduino rovers to drone swarm simulations. My philosophy is simple: build it, break it, learn why, and rebuild it better.
