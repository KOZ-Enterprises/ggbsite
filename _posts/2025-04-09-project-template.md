I've been working on improving the structure and design of my website to make it more user-friendly and visually appealing. Here's what I accomplished:

## What I Did
- Created a **project template** to organize project details, updates, and resources in a clean and structured way.
- Added an **update list** at the top of the project pages to allow users to quickly navigate to specific updates.
- Used **Font Awesome icons** to make the update lists visually appealing and consistent across the site.
- Ensured the design feels **uncluttered** by using proper spacing, colors, and typography.

## Challenges and Solutions
One of the challenges was ensuring the sticky navbar didn't get overlapped by the content when scrolling. I fixed this by adjusting the `z-index` and adding a background color to the navbar. Another challenge was making the update lists visually appealing while keeping them simple and easy to navigate. Using Font Awesome icons and consistent styling helped achieve this.

## Next Steps
- Continue refining the project template to include more features like tags or categories.
- Explore ways to dynamically generate update lists using JavaScript for easier maintenance.
- Test the design on different devices to ensure responsiveness and usability.
