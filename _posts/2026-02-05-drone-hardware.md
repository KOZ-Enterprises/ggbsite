The carbon fiber frames have arrived. I've mounted the motors and ESCs for the first two units.
Using a Raspberry Pi 5 Compute Module for the high-level brain, communicating with a custom flight controller via UART.

Initial motor spin tests look good, but I need to 3D print a mount for the optical flow sensor to ensure stability in GPS-denied environments.
