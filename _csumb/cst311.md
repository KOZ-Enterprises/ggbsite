Survey of Telecomm and Data Comm Technology Fundamentals, Local Area Network, Wide Area Network, Internet and
internetworking protocols including TCP/IP, network security and performance, emerging industry trends such as voice
over the network and high-speed networking. Designed as a foundation for students who wish to pursue more advanced
network studies including certificate programs. Includes hands-on networking labs that incorporate Cisco CCNA lab
components.
