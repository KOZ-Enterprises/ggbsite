<h3>Project Summary: SuperUber Ride-Sharing Application</h3>

<p>SuperUber is a mobile-based ride-sharing platform designed to streamline on-demand transportation by connecting customers and drivers through a robust, cloud-hosted backend. The system manages the entire ride lifecycle—from initial request and real-time price estimation to arrival, pickup, and completion—while integrating external GPS and payment services for seamless operation.</p>

<p><strong>Key points:</strong></p>

<ul>
    <li>Designed a full ride lifecycle supporting real-time matching, tracking, and final fare computation.</li>
    <li>Implemented mutual rating systems and automated monitoring to ensure safety and accountability.</li>
    <li>Architected a data model around six core entities: Customer, Driver, Ride, Rating, Tip, and DissatisfactionReport.</li>
    <li>Integrated external APIs for GPS mapping, payment processing, and status notifications.</li>
</ul>
