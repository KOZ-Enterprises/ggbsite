<h3>Project Summary: Satellite Identifier</h3>

<p>This project investigates the relationship between a satellite’s physical characteristics and its intended mission by developing a predictive model to categorize satellites into six primary classes. By utilizing multinomial Logistic Regression on orbital parameters and physical features, the study demonstrated that specific mission types, such as communications or science, are strongly tied to distinct orbital patterns.</p>

<p><strong>Key points:</strong></p>

<ul>
    <li>Developed a predictive model to categorize satellites with a 94.4% accuracy rate.</li>
    <li>Analyzed orbital parameters like inclination, apogee, and eccentricity alongside launch mass.</li>
    <li>Utilized merged datasets from the UCS Satellite Database and Celestrak SatCat.</li>
    <li>Demonstrated the efficacy of using public orbital data as a proxy for identifying satellite purposes.</li>
</ul>
