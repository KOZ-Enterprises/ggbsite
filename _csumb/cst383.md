Introduction to Data Science provided the skills to utilize statistical programming languages and libraries to preprocess data, apply machine learning models, and visualize complex trends. The course focused on the entire data science pipeline, from acquisition and cleaning to predictive modeling and communicating results through clear visuals. Transitioning from raw data to actionable insights felt like learning a new language, one that allows me to predict the future instead of just reporting the past.
