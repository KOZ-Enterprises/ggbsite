<h3>Satellite Identifier</h3>

<p>This project investigates the relationship between a satellite’s physical characteristics and its intended mission by developing a predictive model to categorize satellites into six primary classes, such as Communications, Navigation, and Science. Utilizing a merged dataset from the UCS Satellite Database and Celestrak SatCat, the study employed multinomial Logistic Regression to analyze orbital parameters like inclination, apogee, and eccentricity alongside physical features like launch mass. The model achieved a high accuracy of 94.4%, demonstrating that specific mission types are strongly tied to distinct orbital patterns—most notably the concentration of communications satellites in geostationary orbits versus science missions in low Earth orbits. Ultimately, the research illustrates that publicly available orbital data serves as a powerful proxy for identifying satellite purposes, offering a scalable method for rapid cataloging and engineering insight.</p>
