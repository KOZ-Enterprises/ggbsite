Capstone Project Planning provided the space to draft a comprehensive proposal for my senior capstone, focusing on project planning, collaboration, and professional communication skills. I worked closely with my advisor to define a substantial technical challenge and outline a roadmap for its successful design and implementation. Planning isn't just a precursor to building—it's the foundation that prevents a project from collapsing under its own weight.
