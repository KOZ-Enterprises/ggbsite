Computer Science Capstone provided the culminating experience of integrating my entire academic journey into a final project that solves a real-world problem. This experience involves exploring the problem space, developing technical specifications, and managing the full design-to-development cycle in a collaborative environment. The capstone is more than just a final project; it's the bridge between being a student and becoming a full-fledged engineer ready for the industry.
