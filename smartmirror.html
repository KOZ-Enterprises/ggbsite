Smart Mirror

The Smart Mirror is a functional mirror that overlays useful widgets like time, weather, and calendar notifications using a Raspberry Pi and a custom display frame.


Project Overview:

  • Status: Completed
  • Objective: Build a smart mirror for daily-use information at a glance.
  • Technologies Used: Raspberry Pi, MagicMirror², Node.js, CSS, HDMI display

Reflection:

This was one of my first DIY tech builds and helped me understand the basics of Linux, web-based UIs, and integrating hardware into a polished product.
